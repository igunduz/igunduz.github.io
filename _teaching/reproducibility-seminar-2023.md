I was a teaching assistant for this seminar in the winter term 2023/24.

About the seminar
======

* A block seminar for Bioinformatics students, run jointly by five groups at Saarland University (Keller, Volkamer, Rahmann, Müller and Gurevich).
* Students worked in groups of two and reproduced figures and results from a published paper.
* Each participant wrote a summary of the assigned paper, gave a talk and handed in an essay.
* The papers assigned by the ICBB lab were single-cell methods, among them MultiVI, SCENIC+ and SEACells.
